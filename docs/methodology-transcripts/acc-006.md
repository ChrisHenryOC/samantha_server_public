# Worked-example transcript — ACC-006 (numeric tier)

**Tier.** 2 — numeric threshold with compound predicate
([decision-gate.md § "Worked-example candidates"](../rule-breakdown/decision-gate.md#worked-example-candidates)).

**Methodology.** [`rule-authoring-methodology.md`](../rule-authoring-methodology.md)

**Date.** 2026-05-05. **Session.** Claude Code (Opus 4.7); methodology

[`decision-gate.md` § "Final primitive set"](../rule-breakdown/decision-gate.md#final-primitive-set)

## 1. Input prompt

Given to the assistant as the authoring task. Quoted as delivered.

> **Task.** Author a deterministic rule encoding the following SOP
> fragment from `knowledge_base/rules/fixation_requirements.md`
> "ACC-006 — Fixation Time Out of Tolerance":
>
> > "HER2 testing requires formalin fixation between 6 and 72 hours
> > inclusive. Orders for the Breast IHC Panel implicitly include
> > HER2. If an order requests HER2 (directly or via the Breast IHC
> > Panel) and `fixation_time_hours` falls outside `[6, 72]`, reject
> > the order — the stain is not interpretable and rerunning is not
> > an option."
>
> **Inputs available.**
>
> - The 9-primitive vocabulary (`IsNull`, `Equals`, `ThresholdGTE`,
>   `ThresholdLTE`, `InEnum`, `Contains`, `BooleanAnd`, `BooleanOr`,
>   `Not`) with the null-discipline contract.
> - The dispatch contract (`step`, `applies_at`, `event_type`).
> - Field shapes on `Order` (see `samantha_server/models/context.py`).
> - Outcome verb-prefix conventions
>   (`docs/rules/conventions.md`).
> - Inventory entry for ACC-006 in `docs/rule-breakdown/inventory.md`
>   (covering scenarios SC-013, SC-014, SC-081, SC-082).
>
> **Output required.** A YAML file at
> `samantha_server/rules/specs/ACC-006.yaml` whose `RuleSpec`
> round-trips via the loader and routes the four covering scenarios so
> that `applied_rules` contains `"ACC-006"`.

## 2. Assistant transcript

### 2.1 Reasoning — fragment classification

The SOP sentence has two trigger conditions joined by AND:

1. The order requests HER2 — either directly (`HER2` in `ordered_tests`)
   or transitively via the `Breast IHC Panel`.
2. `fixation_time_hours` is outside the closed interval `[6, 72]`.

Both are read off the order body at intake; no event-time indirection,
no cross-cutting concerns, no test-conditional branches. The rule's
verdict is `REJECT` because the SOP says "rerunning is not an option"
(unrecoverable).

### 2.2 Tool call — read `Order` to confirm field names and shapes

```text
Read samantha_server/models/context.py
```

Result (excerpt):

```python
class Order(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(frozen=True, ...)
    order_id: str
    ordered_tests: tuple[str, ...]
    fixation_time_hours: float | None
    fixative: Literal["formalin", "NBF", ...] | None
    ...
```

Two fields on the order body: `ordered_tests: tuple[str, ...]`
(matches `Contains`'s container contract; the tuple is frozen-by-
construction because `Order` is `frozen=True`) and
`fixation_time_hours: float | None` (matches `Threshold*`'s scalar
contract). The `| None` matters — see § 2.5 below.

### 2.3 Tool call — primitive selection (HER2-presence half)

The catalog turn produced this composition for the trigger:

```text
SelectPrimitive(
  BooleanOr(
    Contains(field="ordered_tests", value="HER2"),
    Contains(field="ordered_tests", value="Breast IHC Panel"),
  )
)
```

Justification, recorded in the session log:

- The bare shape published in upstream summaries
  ([`decision-gate.md` § "Worked-example
  candidates"](../rule-breakdown/decision-gate.md#worked-example-candidates)
  and [`rule-authoring-methodology.md`
  § 8](../rule-authoring-methodology.md#8-worked-example-transcripts))
  reads `Contains('ordered_tests', 'HER2')`. The canonical YAML is
  broader: it also matches `Breast IHC Panel`, because the panel
  definition implicitly includes HER2 and customers order the panel
  by name far more often than they order HER2 alone. The summary
  documents the *intent* of the trigger; the YAML documents the
  encoding. (Both upstream docs would benefit from a one-line
  reconciliation; tracked as an editorial follow-up.) Folding the
  two cases into a `BooleanOr` keeps the rule firing on real-world
  orders without forcing the engine to expand panel membership at
  runtime.
- An alternative encoding (`Contains('ordered_tests', 'HER2')` only,
  plus a separate "expand panels" preprocessing step) was considered
  and rejected: it would push panel-membership knowledge into the
  context-construction layer, which is the wrong abstraction
  boundary. The rule should read what the order *literally* contains,
  per § 4 step 4 of the methodology ("Verify field naming … `grep`
  the model before authoring"); panel expansion belongs to the order
  itself or is encoded in the predicate, not in a hidden enrichment
  step. Encoding the panel expansion in the rule is the
  ugly-but-honest form.
- A YAML comment is warranted on this branch ("Breast IHC Panel
  implies HER2 evaluation per SOP"); without it a future reviewer
  could reasonably ask why the rule lists two values and would have
  to chase the panel definition to find out.

### 2.4 Tool call — primitive selection (fixation-out-of-tolerance half)

```text
SelectPrimitive(
  BooleanOr(
    Not(ThresholdGTE(field="fixation_time_hours", value=6.0)),
    Not(ThresholdLTE(field="fixation_time_hours", value=72.0)),
  )
)
```

Justification:

- The methodology § 4 dominant-case heuristic prefers naming the
  failure side. Here the failure side is "out of tolerance", which
  is `< 6` OR `> 72`. The 9-primitive set has only `ThresholdGTE` and
  `ThresholdLTE` — there is no strict-inequality primitive — so the
  failure side has no direct encoding. `Not(ThresholdGTE(field, 6))`
  is the documented form for `< 6`, and `Not(ThresholdLTE(field, 72))`
  for `> 72`.
- The methodology § 4 step 2 worked example for ACC-006 anticipates
  this:
  > "For numeric ranges, compose two `Threshold` primitives under
  > `BooleanAnd` (in-tolerance) or `BooleanOr` of two negations
  > (out-of-tolerance) — see ACC-006."
  The `BooleanOr`-of-negations form is the documented exception to
  the "avoid `Not(...)` wrappers" rule because the underlying
  primitive set forces it. Per
  [`decision-gate.md` § "Final primitive set"](../rule-breakdown/decision-gate.md#final-primitive-set)
  the irreducible-`Not(...)` cases in the corpus are ACC-006,
  IHC-001 (the threshold-disjunction mirror), and ACC-008
  (negation-over-disjunction).
- An equivalent form `Not(BooleanAnd(ThresholdGTE(.., 6),
  ThresholdLTE(.., 72)))` was considered and rejected. By
  De Morgan, it's logically identical, but it pushes the negation up
  one level, away from the primitive that actually fails — which
  hurts readability of receipts. The published convention names the
  *individual* threshold violation, so the receipt records "the
  `ThresholdGTE` half failed" rather than "some compound check
  failed".

### 2.5 Reasoning — null semantics on `fixation_time_hours`

The order body allows `fixation_time_hours: None` (e.g., a freshly
received order before fixation logging). Per
[`decision-gate.md` § "Null semantics rationale"](../rule-breakdown/decision-gate.md):

- `ThresholdGTE(None, 6.0)` returns `False` (fail-closed).
- `Not(False)` returns `True`.
- So `Not(ThresholdGTE('fixation_time_hours', 6.0))` evaluates to
  `True` when `fixation_time_hours is None`.

The SOP fragment quoted in the prompt ("falls outside `[6, 72]`")
does not literally cover the missing-value case; the verdict here is
reached by the kernel-wide null-discipline policy, not derived from
the SOP text. The policy direction matches what one would *want* a
HER2 order with no recorded fixation time to do — reject rather than
silently proceed — so the rule needs no explicit `IsNull` guard. The
fail-closed null discipline lines up with the SOP's intent without
extra guards. (IHC-001 is the publication-quality artifact for this
invariant; flagged in the annotations rather than re-derived here.)

### 2.6 Tool call — dispatcher fields

```text
SetDispatch(step="ACCESSIONING", applies_at=None, event_type="order_received")
```

- `step="ACCESSIONING"` — the SOP fragment is in the accessioning
  bundle (`accessioning.md` cross-references the fixation-requirements
  page; both list ACC-006 per `inventory.md` § "SOP source coverage").
- `applies_at=None` — non-IHC rules carry `null` here (methodology
  § 2). ACC-006 does not depend on a workflow phase entry; it
  evaluates at order intake.
- `event_type="order_received"` — both `ordered_tests` and
  `fixation_time_hours` are order-body fields; the only event whose
  payload presents the order shape pre-validation is
  `order_received`.

### 2.7 Tool call — action and severity

```text
SetAction(
  transition="DO_NOT_PROCESS",
  severity="REJECT",
  outcome="rejected_fixation_out_of_tolerance",
)
```

- `severity="REJECT"` — the SOP says "reject the order" and "rerunning
  is not an option" (unrecoverable).
- `transition="DO_NOT_PROCESS"` — the canonical accessioning terminal
  state for `REJECT` severity (verified against
  `samantha_server/models/context.py:VALID_STATES`).
- `outcome="rejected_fixation_out_of_tolerance"` follows the
  `rejected_*` prefix for `REJECT` severity (`docs/rules/conventions.md`
  § "outcome verb-prefix convention"). The suffix names the *cause*,
  not the trigger ("fixation_out_of_tolerance" rather than
  "her2_with_bad_fixation"); the cause is what an auditor reading the
  receipt needs.

### 2.8 Emitted YAML

```yaml

step: ACCESSIONING
applies_at: null
event_type: order_received
severity: REJECT
priority: null
when:
  boolean_and:
    - boolean_or:
        # Breast IHC Panel implies HER2 evaluation per SOP. If the panel
        # definition ever changes (e.g., DCIS-only variants), revisit this branch.
        # See knowledge_base/sops/<panel>.md (samantha-public).
        - contains:
            field: ordered_tests
            value: HER2
        - contains:
            field: ordered_tests
            value: Breast IHC Panel
    - boolean_or:
        - not:
            threshold_gte:
              field: fixation_time_hours
              value: 6.0
        - not:
            threshold_lte:
              field: fixation_time_hours
              value: 72.0

  transition: DO_NOT_PROCESS
  set_flags: []
  clear_flags: []
  outcome: rejected_fixation_out_of_tolerance
  panel: null
  branch: null

```

## 3. Annotations

### 3.1 What worked

- **The methodology's "compose two thresholds" line resolved the
  range encoding immediately.** The instinct on first read was
  `Not(BooleanAnd(GTE 6, LTE 72))` (a single outer negation reads
  more "obvious"). Methodology § 4 step 2's worked example pre-empted
  that with the explicit `BooleanOr` of two `Not(...)` wrappers, on
  the grounds that the receipt needs to name the individual
  threshold that failed. The choice was made in one tool turn, with
  the De Morgan-equivalent form recorded as a rejected alternative
  rather than re-litigated.
- **The convention table named the outcome unambiguously.** The
  `REJECT → rejected_*` row in `docs/rules/conventions.md` plus the
  receipt-readability heuristic (name the cause, not the trigger)
  produced `rejected_fixation_out_of_tolerance` on the first pass.
  Candidates like `rejected_her2_bad_fixation` or `bad_fixation`
  never entered the tool stream.
- **Null fail-closed semantics aligned with the SOP intent without
  any guard predicate.** A naïve author might add an `IsNull`
  short-circuit ("if `fixation_time_hours` is null, reject") on the
  assumption that the threshold primitives ignore nulls.
  Methodology § 1's null-discipline paragraph plus the worked
  derivation in § 2.5 above showed the rule already does the right
  thing — `Not(ThresholdGTE(None, 6))` is `True`, which is the
  desired verdict. No guard needed; one fewer branch in the tree.

### 3.2 What required correction

- **Initial trigger was too narrow.** First pass landed
  `Contains('ordered_tests', 'HER2')` directly, matching the bare
  shape published in the methodology summary and the decision-gate
  worked-example column. The SOP fragment explicitly extends the
  trigger to the `Breast IHC Panel` ("orders for the Breast IHC
  Panel implicitly include HER2"); the bare-`Contains` form would
  miss every panel-keyed order in the corpus. A re-read of the SOP
  promoted the predicate to `BooleanOr(Contains 'HER2',
  Contains 'Breast IHC Panel')`. The published one-line summary is
  *the predicate's intent*, not the predicate itself — a worth
  remembering for any rule with panel/test aliasing.
- **YAML comment placement on the panel-OR branch.** The rule reads
  cleanly without the comment, but a future reviewer looking at the
  raw spec would have to chase the panel definition to understand
  why two values appear. The comment was added inside the
  `boolean_or` block on the first edit; the only correction was its
  scope — the initial draft commented the *outer* `BooleanAnd`,
  which read as "this whole rule is about HER2 panels", obscuring
  that the fixation-tolerance half is the actual decision. Moving
  the comment one level in fixed the drift.
- **`event_type` briefly considered as `intake_validation`.**
  The intake-validation event was a tempting target ("isn't the
  fixation tolerance check a validation?"), but the dispatch
  contract binds on the *event whose payload carries the fields the
  rule reads*. `intake_validation` doesn't carry the order body;
  `order_received` does. Methodology § 2's collision-risk note was
  enough to flip the choice without a separate re-read.

### 3.3 What surprised

- **The irreducible `Not(...)` wrapper is the documented exception,
  not a smell.** The "name the dominant case; outer `Not(...)` is a
  smell" guidance is strong, and on the first pass it produced a
  tool turn questioning whether the threshold primitives were the
  wrong choice (could `InEnum(fixation_band, ['out'])` work after a
  preprocessing step?). The decision-gate's primitive-set rationale
  resolved the doubt: the 9-primitive set deliberately omits
  strict-inequality primitives because the `Not(GTE(.., x))`
  composition is rare enough not to justify a tenth. ACC-006 is one
  of three rules in the entire corpus that hits this exception
  (per [`decision-gate.md` § "Final primitive
  set"](../rule-breakdown/decision-gate.md#final-primitive-set):
  ACC-006, IHC-001's threshold mirror, and ACC-008's
  negation-over-disjunction). The other fixation-family rules —
  ACC-005 (`Not(Equals(fixative,'formalin'))`) and ACC-009
  (`IsNull(fixation_time_hours)`) — share the outer
  `BooleanAnd(HER2-trigger, fixation-check)` skeleton with ACC-006
  but use different inner predicates and don't hit the
  threshold-disjunction shape. The "smell" framing is a corpus-level
  heuristic, not an axiom — and the methodology should perhaps be
  more explicit that this particular `Not(...)` shape is **the
  exception to plan around**, not an authoring miss.
- **The covering scenarios are bimodal.** SC-013 and SC-014 isolate
  ACC-006 with under- and over-tolerance fixation times — they're
  the rule's red/green proof. SC-081 and SC-082 are *multi-rule*
  scenarios where ACC-006 is one of several violations. The
  rule-correctness criterion is "ACC-006 appears in `applied_rules`",
  not "the scenario terminates at any specific state" — exactly
  the same lesson ACC-001's transcript surfaces with SC-008. A
  novice author hunting for a counterexample to the rule could
  mis-read SC-082 (five rules fire, one terminal state) as evidence
  that ACC-006's predicate is wrong; the receipt log is what makes
  "ACC-006 fired alongside four other rules; severity hierarchy
  picked the verdict" auditable.
- **The panel-membership comment is load-bearing for review, not
  for the engine.** The engine doesn't read YAML comments. But the
  reviewer does, and the comment is the only artifact on the spec
  that says *why* `Breast IHC Panel` appears alongside `HER2`. If
  the panel definition ever changes (DCIS-only variants, panel
  rename), the comment is the breadcrumb that says "revisit this
  branch". Treating the comment as part of the rule's documented
  surface (rather than a throwaway annotation) is a tier-2
  observation that ACC-001 didn't surface — trivial-tier rules
  rarely need a comment at all.
